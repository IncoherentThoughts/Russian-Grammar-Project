УРО́К НО́МЕР ПЯТЬ5 Asking Questions; The Prepositional Case

5.1 Phrases

Где вокза́л?
Where's the station?
Когда́ рабо́тает музе́й?
When is the museum open (When works the museum)?
Почему́ рестора́н не рабо́тает?
Why is the restaurant closed?
Кто э́то?
Who is that?
Пря́мо и напра́во.
Straight on and turn right.
Они́ в Сиби́ри.
They're in Siberia.

5.2 Asking Wh-Questions

Wh-questions are questions with question words such as ‘what’, ‘who’, ‘when’, ‘why’, ‘how’. You already know где ‘where’, что ‘what’, почему́ ‘why’. Когда́ is ‘when’.

Когда́ рабо́тает рестора́н?
When is the restaurant open?
Кто зна́ет?
Who knows?

Often the emphatic particle же is added after the question word: же emphasizes the previous word, so the effect is like pronouncing ‘Where is she?’ with extra emphasis on ‘is’.

5.3 Yes-No Questions1

These are questions which expect the answer ‘yes’ or ‘no’. In Russian they look the same as statements, apart from the question mark. In the spoken language, it is intonation which distinguishes Э́то во́дка? from Э́то во́дка. Practise the intonation, making sure that your voice goes up and down on the stressed syllable of the word you are asking about:

Вы зна́ете?
Do you know?
Э́то ко́фе?
Is this coffee?
Он англича́нин?
Is he English?
1 Pronunciation note. Russian prepositions are read as if they are joined to the following word. So в [v] sounds like [f] before unvoiced consonants. See 2.3 and 2.8.

5.4 Prepositional Case

In Lesson 4 we met the question ‘Where do you live?’ Где вы живёте? (see 4.6). The Russian for ‘in’ is в, but after в meaning ‘in’, following nouns have an ending (usually -е) called the prepositional case. These prepositions are:

в
in
на
on
о
about, concerning
при (fairly rare)
attached to; in the presence of

Nouns which end with a consonant add -е; nouns which end -й, -а or -о change the vowel to -е.

Ло́ндон → в Ло́ндоне
in London
музе́й → в музе́е
in the museum
Москва́ → в Москве́
in Moscow
письмо́ → в письме́
in a/the letter

5.12 Dialogues

1А: Где Москва́? — Б: В Росси́и.

2А: Где Ло́ндон? — Б: В А́нглии.

3А: Где Ки́ев? — Б: На Украи́не.

Translations

1A: Where is Moscow? — B: In Russia.

2A: Where is London? — B: In England.

3A: Where is Kiev? — B: In the Ukraine.
