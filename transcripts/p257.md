### 20.7 EXTRA: Ходи́ть/идти́ with Vehicles

_As a general rule_, vehicles, particularly large ones such as trains, boats, buses and trams, go 'on foot' (**ходи́ть/идти́**). In particular, **ходи́ть/идти́** is normal when you are talking about the route of a vehicle or its timetable, as in the first three examples below.

Куда́ <u>идёт</u> э́тот авто́бус? — Where does this bus go?
Когда́ <u>отхо́дит</u> по́езд? — When does the train leave?
Такси́ <u>идёт</u> в парк. — The taxi is going to the depot (it's the end of the driver's shift).
По́езд вы<u>хо́дит</u> из тунне́ля. — The train is coming out of the tunnel.
Во́т <u>идёт</u> трамва́й. — There's a tram coming.
Теплохо́д вы́<u>шел</u> из га́вани. — The steamer left the harbour.
