But if they do not end **-а, -я** or **-ь**, they are indeclinable (see 5.10).

### 3.9 It

When you're talking about something which has already been named, 'it' will be **о́н/она́/оно́**, depending on the gender of the noun to which 'it' refers.

A: Где чай? — Where is the tea?
B: Во́т о́н. — There it ('he') is.
A: Где вино́? — Where is the wine?
B: Во́т оно́. — There it is.
