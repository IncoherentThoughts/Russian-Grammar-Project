### 10.12 Accusative Plural of Animates = Genitive Plural

In 8/2 it was mentioned that all _animate_ nouns (people and animals) of whatever gender have special endings in the accusative plural. These endings, and the endings of any accompanying adjectives, possessives and demonstratives, are the _same as the endings of the genitive plural_.

Я люблю́ ру́сских де́вушек. — I love Russian girls.
  ру́сск<u>их</u> де́вуш<u>ек</u> also means 'of Russian girls'
Мы зна́ем э́т<u>их</u> америка́нц<u>ев</u>. — We know these Americans.
  э́тих америка́нцев also means 'of these Americans'
Е́ва лю́бит англи́йск<u>их</u> соба́к_. — Eva loves English dogs.

### 10.13 Adjectives with Numerals

There are no surprises with оди́н or numerals which are followed by the genitive plural:

Я зна́ю шестьдеся́т одно́ ру́сск<u>ое</u> (nom.sg.) сло́во (nom.sg.) — I know 61 Russian words
пять но́в<u>ых</u> (gen.pl.) сло́в (gen.pl.) — five new words

_But_ after два, три, четы́ре, nouns are in the _genitive singular_ (9.5) and any adjectives are in the _genitive plural_ with masculine and neuter nouns and in the _nominative plural_ with feminine ones:

два иностра́нн<u>ых</u> (gen.pl.) языка́ (gen.sg.) — two foreign languages
