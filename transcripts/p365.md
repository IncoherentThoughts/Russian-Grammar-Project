### 29.4 Past Active Participles (Who/Which VERB-ed/was VERB-ing

These participles have the characteristic ending **-вший**, an adjective ending which agrees in number, gender and case with the noun to which it relates. It means 'who (or which) VERB-ed or was VERB-ing'.

**изуча́вший**<sup>i</sup> — who was studying
**изучи́вший**<sup>p</sup> — who mastered

They are formed from both imperfective and perfective verbs using this rule: take off the **-л** of the past tense and add **-вший**:

**изуча́ть**<sup>i</sup> 'to study' (past **изуча́л**) – **изуча́вший**:

**Студе́нты, изуча́<u>вшие</u>**<sup>i</sup> **ру́сский язы́к, нашли́ интере́сную рабо́ту.**
The students who studied Russian found interesting work.

**изучи́ть**<sup>p</sup> 'to master' (past **изучи́л**) – **изучи́вший**:

**Бизнесме́нов, изучи́<u>вших</u>**<sup>p</sup> (gen.pl) **ру́сский язы́к, о́чень ма́ло.**
There are very few businessmen who have mastered Russian.

If the verb is reflexive, add **-ся** (never **-сь**).

**занима́ться**<sup>i</sup> 'to study' (past **занима́лся**) – **занима́вшийся**:

**Все де́вушки, занима́вшиеся**<sup>i</sup> **ру́сским языко́м, вы́шли за́муж за ру́сских.**
All the girls who studied Russian married Russians.
