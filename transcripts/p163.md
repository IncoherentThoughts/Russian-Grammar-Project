Джо́н спроси́л: «А́нна говори́т (Говори́т ли А́нна) по-ру́сски?»
John asked: 'Does Anna speak Russian?'

Report of John's question:
Джо́н спроси́л, говор<u>и́т</u> (pres.) <u>ли</u> А́нна по-ру́сски.
John asked if Anna _spoke_ (past) Russian.

More examples:

I asked if (=whether) Boris was at home. (Actual question: 'Is Boris at home?')
Я спроси́ла, до́ма ли Бори́с (present tense).

The reported question Я спроси́ла, был ли Бори́с до́ма would correspond to 'I asked if Boris _had been_ at home.'

This same construction with ли is used in all structures involving 'whether' or 'if' in the meaning 'whether'.

I didn't know if (=whether) she would come. (Actual thoughts: 'I didn't know if she _will_ come.')
Я не знал, придёт (future) ли она́.
