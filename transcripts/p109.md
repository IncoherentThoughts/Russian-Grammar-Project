### 9.10 One: оди́н etc. (Table 7)

Note that one (оди́н, одна́, одно́ etc.) is not a numeral (i.e. it is not a quantity word followed by the genitive case). It behaves like an adjective, agreeing with the following noun, and has the same endings as э́тот, э́та, э́то 'this'. The и of од(и́)н is a mobile vowel (8.6).

Я зна́ю одну́ де́вушку. — I know one girl (acc.).
Я зна́ю одного́ америка́нца. — I know one American (animate acc.).
