Вас is the accusative case of вы 'you'; the details of the accusative are in Lesson 6.

These three sets of similar endings in 4.4–4.6 give the present tense of nearly all Russian verbs. These endings don't take long to learn; the main problem is remembering the stem (and the stress pattern) of verbs like е́хать, жить and звать. Although most verbs are like знать or говори́ть, there are many irregular infinitives like жить. In the vocabularies, we give the я and the ты form of each verb (since you can work out the other forms from these two).

### 4.7 Verb Summary Table

| | знать (1) | говори́ть (2) | жить (1B) | е́хать (1B) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Infinitive | знать (1) | говори́ть (2) | жить (1B) | е́хать (1B) |
| Stem | зна- | говор- | жив- | е́д- |
| я | -ю | -ю́ | -у́ | -у |
| ты | -ешь | -и́шь | -ёшь | -ешь |
| о́н/она́/оно́ | -ет | -и́т | -ёт | -ет |
| мы | -ем | -и́м | -ём | -ем |
| вы | -ете | -и́те | -ёте | -ете |
| они́ | -ют | -я́т | -у́т | -ут |

Stress notes. (1) A few знать-type verbs are stressed on the end, so each е turns into ё. An example is дава́ть 'to give' (12.4), which has the stem да-. The stressed endings are: даю́, даёшь, даёт, даём, даёте, даю́т. (2) Many говори́ть-type verbs are stressed on the stem (see 4.9). but the spelling is not affected.

### 4.8 Negation ('not')

To make a verb negative ('I don't know') simply put не 'not' in front of the verb:

Я не зна́ю. — I don't know.
О́н не понима́ет. — He doesn't understand.
