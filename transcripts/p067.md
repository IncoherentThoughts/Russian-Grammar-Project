### 6.7 Accusative Case of Pronouns

Just as 'me' is the accusative of 'I', so меня́ is the accusative of я. Here is the full list of personal accusative pronouns:

| Nominative | | Accusative | |
|---|---|---|---|
| я | I | меня́ | me |
| ты | you | тебя́ | you |
| о́н | he | его́ [ye-vó] | him |
| она́ | she | её | her |
| оно́ | it | его́ [ye-vó] | it |
| мы | we | нас | us |
| вы | you | вас | you |
| они́ | they | их | them |

### 6.8 Uses of the Accusative

(1) After verbs, where you would use 'him' not 'he' in English ('I know him' not 'I know he'):

Я зна́ю Ива́на. — I know Ivan.
Вы зна́ете его́ [ye-vó]? — Do you know him?

(2) In time expressions of duration and frequency:

Подожди́те мину́ту. — Wait _a minute_.
Я здесь уже́ неде́лю. — I've been here _for a week_. (literally 'I am here already a week' – неде́ля 'week')
