### 9.6 Genitive Plural (abbreviated 'gen.pl.')

This is the most complex ending in Russian. The five main rules are given here. Other, less important, rules are given in the 'EXTRA' section. The genitive plural form of awkward nouns (e.g. копе́йка – копе́ек 'of kopecks') is given in the vocabularies.

(a) All nouns ending -ь, _whether m or f_, replace the soft sign with -ей (which is often stressed, i.e. -е́й, particularly if the noun is short, but the stress is hard to predict).

рубль (m) 'rouble' — рубле́й 'of roubles'
д(е)нь (m) 'day' — дне́й 'of days'
пло́щадь (f) 'square' — площаде́й 'of squares'
жи́тель (m) 'inhabitant' — жи́телей 'of inhabitants'

Remember that мать and до́чь always have -ер- before endings:

мать 'mother' — матере́й 'of mothers'
до́чь 'daughter' — дочере́й 'of daughters'

(b) Nouns ending -ж, -ч, -ш, -щ add -ей (as in (a), often stressed -е́й):

эта́ж 'floor' — этаже́й 'of floors'
врач 'doctor' — враче́й 'of doctors'
това́рищ 'comrade' — това́рищей 'of comrades'
