## 19 The Conditional; Obligation (до́лжен); Prefixes
_УРО́К НО́МЕР ДЕВЯТНА́ДЦАТЬ_

### 19.1 Key Phrases

Е́сли бы я знал, я ушёл бы. — If I'd known, I would have left.
Я бы хоте́л(а) вас пригласи́ть. — I would like to invite you.
Я до́лжен/должна́ извини́ться. — I must apologize.
Мы должны́ бы́ли отказа́ться. — We had to refuse.
Проходи́те. — Go through.
Вхо́д/вы́ход/перехо́д/ухо́д — Entrance/exit/crossing/departure

### 19.2 Conditional Mood

Е́сли <u>бы</u> я жи<u>л</u>(а́) в Росси́и, я <u>бы</u> говори́<u>л</u>(а) по-ру́сски. — If I lived in Russia, I would speak Russian.

The equivalent of 'would' in Russian is a particle бы, which suggests a hypothesis or something contrary to fact or something which you would like to happen. Бы has no meaning of its own and is never used without other words; it cannot occur first in the sentence or clause. It adds a meaning of hypothesis, doubt or wish to the verb with which it is used. So о́н был means 'he was' while о́н был бы means 'he would be' or 'he would have been'.
