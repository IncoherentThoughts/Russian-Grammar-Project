### 4.11 Prepositional Case

As an example of the way nouns change their form, look at these sentences:

Я живу́ в Бра́йтоне. — I live in Brighton.
О́н живёт в Москве́. — He lives in Moscow.
Я е́ду на авто́бусе. — I'm going on a bus (by bus).

After the prepositions в 'in' and на 'on', most masculine nouns _add_ -е and most feminine nouns _change_ their last -а or -я to -е.

Москва́ 'Moscow' — в Москве́ 'in Moscow'
Бра́йтон 'Brighton' — в Бра́йтоне 'in Brighton'
авто́бус 'bus' — на авто́бусе 'on a bus'

There are more details of these case endings in Lesson 5.
