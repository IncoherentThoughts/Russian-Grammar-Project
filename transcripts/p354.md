### 28.6 Perfective Verbal Adverb (PVA)

The closest English equivalent of the perfective verbal adverb (PVA) is 'having Verb-ed', e.g. 'having said'. PVAs are formed from perfective verbs and have the characteristic endings **-в**, or, for reflexive verbs, **-вшись**.

**Formation**

If the infinitive ends **-ть**, replace the **ть** with **в**:

сказа́ть<sup>p</sup> 'to say' — сказа́в 'having said'
дать<sup>p</sup> 'to give' — дав 'having given'

If the infinitive does not end **-ть**, form the PVA using the rules for the _imperfective_ VA, i.e. replace the third-person plural ending with **-я**:

уйти́<sup>p</sup> 'to go away' они́ уйду́т: уйд + я = уйдя́ 'having left'
вы́йти<sup>p</sup> 'to go out' они́ вы́йдут: вы́йд + я = вы́йдя 'having gone out'
принести́<sup>p</sup> 'to bring' они́ принесу́т: принес + я = принеся́ 'having brought'
