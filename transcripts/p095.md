adjectives ending -цый, but should you ever meet ку́цый 'tailless' or красноли́цый 'red-faced', the neuter forms will be ку́цее and красноли́цее.

_Note._ Rule 2 does _not_ apply to stressed о́, so больш<u>о́</u>й 'big' has о, not е, after ш. However, большо́й is unique. There are no other adjectives ending -шо́й, and none ending -жо́й, -чо́й, -що́й or -цо́й.
