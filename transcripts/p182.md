Бу́дьте добры́. — Be so good. (Set phrase introducing a request)

This ending -ь(те) is by far the least common of the three.

### EXERCISE 15/3

Make the infinitives imperative:

1 (Оста́ться<sup>p</sup> 14.7) здесь 'Stay here.' 2 Пожа́луйста, (отве́тить<sup>p</sup>) на два вопро́са 'Please answer two questions.' 3 Ва́ня, не (забы́ть<sup>p</sup>) позвони́ть Е́ве 'Vania, don't forget to ring Eva.'

### 15.3 Choice of Aspect

**Rule 1**: Use the _perfective_ for commands and requests involving single events:

Скажи́те<sup>p</sup>, пожа́луйста . . . — Could you tell me . . .
Позови́те<sup>p</sup>, пожа́луйста, Е́ву. — Please call Eva.

**Rule 2**: Use the _imperfective_ for commands to do something repeatedly or without time limitation, as you would expect from the general rules of aspect usage:

Пиши́те<sup>i</sup> мне ча́сто. — Write to me often. (Compare Напиши́те<sup>p</sup> ей письмо́ 'Write her a letter'.)
Говори́те<sup>i</sup> по-ру́сски, пожа́луйста. — Speak Russian please. (Compare Скажи́те<sup>p</sup> э́то по-ру́сски, пожа́луйста 'Say that in Russian, please.')

**Rule 3**: Use the _imperfective_ for negative commands (Don't . . .) with не:

Не уходи́те<sup>i</sup>. — Don't go away.
Не спра́шивайте<sup>i</sup>. — Don't ask.
