### 7.16 Adverbs

Adverbs are words which can fill the gap in a sentence such as 'She speaks Russian ______', e.g. quickly, well, often, impressively. English adverbs are often formed from adjectives by adding -ly (nicely, cleverly, coldly etc.). The commonest adverb ending in Russian is -о, replacing the -ый/-ий/-о́й adjective ending. Adverbs are always indeclinable. Examples:

краси́вый beautiful — краси́во beautifully
интере́сный interesting — интере́сно interestingly
холо́дный cold — хо́лодно coldly (NB stress)
хоро́ший good (see Lesson 8.10) — хорошо́ well (NB stress)
  Она́ говори́т хорошо́. — She speaks well.
