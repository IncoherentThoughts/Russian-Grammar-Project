  с вокза́л<u>а</u> — from the station
у — by, near; at (someone's place), _chez_
  у окн<u>а́</u> — by the window
  у нас — in our house/ in our country/ at our place

### 10.8 EXTRA: Partitive ('Some') Genitive

A minor use of the genitive case is to express the meaning 'some' with food and drink nouns:

Да́йте вод<u>ы́</u>/вин<u>а́</u>/хле́б<u>а</u>. — Give me some water/some wine/some bread.
(Да́йте хлеб corresponds to 'Give me bread' or 'Give me the bread'.)

A small number of nouns have a special у/ю ending for the partitive. You may hear the question:

Вы хоти́те ча́ю? — Would you like some tea?

Ча́ю, with a special genitive ending -ю (an alternative to -я), is the partitive genitive of чай 'tea'. Other nouns which _may_ have this -у ending are са́хар 'sugar', мёд 'honey', сыр 'cheese':

ло́жка са́хар<u>у</u> (or са́хар<u>а</u>) — a spoonful of sugar
