## 7 Describing Things: Adjectives
_УРО́К НО́МЕР СЕМЬ_

### 7.1 Phrases with Adjectives

Большо́й теа́тр — Bolshoi (Big) Theatre
ру́сский язы́к — Russian language
До́брый день — Good day
Тверска́я у́лица — Tver' Street (Moscow's main street)
До́брое у́тро — Good morning
в Большо́м теа́тре — in the Bolshoi Theatre
на Кра́сной пло́щади — in (on) Red Square

_Pronunciation note_: the й in the endings -ый and -ий is inaudible, so there's no need to make an effort to pronounce it.

### 7.2 Adjectives

An adjective is a word such as 'good', 'interesting', 'Soviet' which can describe a noun, that is, any word which fits in the gap in a sentence such as 'It's a ______ thing'. In Russian, adjectives have endings which must agree with the noun, that is, if the noun is neuter (у́тро 'morning'), then the adjective must have a neuter ending too (the ending -ое on до́брое in До́брое у́тро 'Good morning').
