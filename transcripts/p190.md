'_War and Peace_' and 'He read _War and Peace_' is a difference of aspect, not of tense (they're both past tense). 'He was reading' tells us that the reading was in process, not finished; 'he read' simply tells us what kind of activity it was – perhaps he finished the book (as in 'He read _War and Peace_ in sixty-five minutes'), perhaps he didn't (as in 'He read _War and Peace_ for a couple of hours and realized he would never finish it').
